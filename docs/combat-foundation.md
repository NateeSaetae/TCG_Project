# PvE combat foundation

`src/data/characters/marth/marthGameplay.ts` is Marth's canonical gameplay record. All six Marth card prints reference the same object through `HeroCardData.gameplay`. Rarity, artwork, Cost and Power remain card presentation data; Power is not used as ATK.

`src/types/combat.ts` defines base stats, compatible weapons, skills, temporary modifiers, teams, turns, actions and events. `src/types/items.ts` keeps future item rarity and upgrade fields separate from character rarity. Characters without complete gameplay data can omit `gameplay` and still render.

`src/combat/engine.ts` is independent of React and uses immutable state updates. It can create a two-team battle, sum living members' effective SPD for provisional initiative, check weapon compatibility, evaluate attack passives, declare an attack, accept externally calculated damage, run on-kill triggers, expire buffs, and detect defeat. `MAG: null` remains unavailable rather than becoming zero.

The caller must supply base damage to `applyResolvedDamage`; the engine adds any damage bonus recorded when the attack was declared. There is no general damage, healing, defense, resistance, action-count, AI or tie-break formula yet. An attack can carry `defenseIgnorePercent`; callers should use `effectiveDefenseForAttack(state, attackId)` when calculating physical damage so this percentage affects the target's DEF. Equal team Speed requires an explicit first team. A buff applied during a team's turn lasts through the end of that team's next turn; the engine tracks completed turns to expire it. These policies are extension points for the future PvE rules.

The Marth Common card uses the canonical record to show allowed weapons, a collapsible status rail, and two skills. Other rarities retain their approved presentation while sharing the same gameplay data.

Roy C–SR share `src/data/characters/roy/royGameplay.ts`. Blazing Resolve adds attack at 50% HP or lower. Binding Flame adds damage to a Sword hit when the target has more current HP than Roy at attack declaration.

Hector and Ephraim each have one gameplay record shared by their registered C-UR prints. Hector's Ostian Guard reduces incoming attack damage by 15%, rounded down to a whole point after damage bonuses; it also applies to attacks without a weapon. Armads Retribution deals 300 damage directly to the attacker after Hector survives a hit, at most once per enemy turn. Direct counter damage never starts another counterattack. Ephraim's Siegmund's Edge records 20% DEF penetration on Lance attacks; Renais Assault adds 300 ATK to an attack declared against a full-HP target. Neither effect permanently changes base stats. The catalog leaves SER unregistered until those characters have distinct SER artwork. Their C-UR Cost and Power values are provisional presentation data.

Lilina and Lyn also share one gameplay record across their C-UR prints. Magic attacks declare effective MAG, so Arcane Flame adds 300 MAG for only that attack. Lyn's Swift Strike compares effective SPD at attack declaration, and Sacaen Precision records 15% DEF penetration for Bow attacks. Bow has a shared card glyph and is checked by weapon compatibility. Staff is healing-only; Gentle Heart is typed with a 300 HP healing bonus and marked `runtime: 'pending'` until the engine supports heal actions, healing amounts, and maximum-HP capping. No final damage formula is defined. Lilina and Lyn SER remain unregistered because their distinct character artwork is not present.
